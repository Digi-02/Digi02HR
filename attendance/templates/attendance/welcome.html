Each company's data is isolated by organization. Role-based access (owner, HR admin, manager, payroll, viewer). Sensitive actions are written to an audit trail. Email delivery is configured via your own SMTP settings.

FAQ

Is DIGI HR really free?

Yes, for now. No plans or subscriptions are offered.

How does attendance work?

Employees open your kiosk link and check in with their work email. Late is flagged after your configured threshold (default 08:30).

Can I upload my company logo?

Yes, in Settings → Company Branding, plus your brand color.

Is my company's data separated?

Yes. Records belong to one organization and staff only see their own workspace.

Does it handle payroll?

It prepares monthly payroll runs and payslips for review and approval.
